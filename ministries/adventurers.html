Why Adventurers

Why every family should join an Adventurer club

Adventurers supports parents as the primary educators and spiritual mentors of their children, fosters growth that is spiritual, physical, mental and social, and leads each child toward a personal commitment to Jesus Christ and a positive attitude to Christian living.

  • auto_stories

    Faith from the start

    Bible stories, songs and simple prayers at the age when children believe with their whole hearts, so knowing Jesus feels natural.

  • family_restroom

    Parents at the center

    Adventurers is a family club. Parents learn alongside their children and take home ideas for worship, play and talking about God.

  • toys

    Learning by doing

    Crafts, games, nature walks and short projects. Every award is earned with hands, eyes and questions, a few meetings at a time.

  • diversity_1

    A place to belong

    Small groups by age, the same friends year after year, and grown-ups at church who know every child by name.

  • emoji_events

    Confidence and character

    Being kind, helpful and true, practiced every week and celebrated with a patch they can point to on their sash.

  • shield_person

    Safe, caring adults

    Every staff member is screened and trained, and parents are always welcome in the room.

For parents

What a club meeting looks like

Meetings are short and active, made for small attention spans and busy hands.

  1. 1music_note

    Welcome and worship

    Songs, a short Bible story and prayer, and everyone says the Pledge and Law together.

  2. 2groups

    Class time

    Children split into their class with a counselor they know and work on the year's requirements.

  3. 3palette

    Award activity

    A craft, game, nature walk or visit that counts toward an award, learned by doing.

  4. 4home

    Closing and take-home

    A closing prayer, and a note for parents with something to talk about or practice at home that week.

What an Adventurer promises

The Adventurer Pledge

“Because Jesus loves me, I will always do my best.”

Adventurers say the Pledge together at every meeting. The Law is ten simple ways Jesus helps them live it, one day at a time.

The growth journey

Six classes, one for every year of early childhood

Each class is built for one school year. Children move up together with the friends they started with, and at the end of the club year they are invested before their family and church.

For parents

Questions families ask

Answers below are drafts for the Director to confirm.

How old does my child need to be?add

Children join the year they turn four (Little Lamb) and stay through age nine (Helping Hand). At ten they move up to Pathfinders.

Do parents have to take part?add

Yes, and that is the point. Adventurers is a family club: a parent or guardian attends with the child and often helps lead an award.

What does it cost?add

Each club sets a small annual fee for materials and insurance. Uniform pieces are bought separately; ask the local director what a new Adventurer needs first.

Is it only for Adventist families?add

No. Any family willing to take part in the program, including its Bible teaching, is welcome.

How to start

Start an Adventurer club at your church

A church can open an Adventurer club with a few committed families. These are the steps in Greater New York; the conference office walks you through each one.

  1. Talk with your pastor and boardDRAFT

    The church board votes to sponsor a club and appoints a director.

  2. Register with the conferenceDRAFT

    Tell the Adventurer office you are starting and register the club for the year.

  3. Build and screen your staffDRAFT

    Every adult completes a background check, child-protection training and Basic Staff Training.

  4. Meet with parentsDRAFT

    Explain the family model, the classes by age, dues and uniform, and invite parents to help lead.

  5. Launch and be commissionedDRAFT

    Hold your first meeting and induct your Adventurers before the church.

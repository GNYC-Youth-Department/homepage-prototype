Judging

How a corps is scored

Judges score musicality, precision, showmanship and uniform, with penalties for time and boundary faults. Confirm the categories and weights with the coordinator.

Questions

Directors ask

person

Drum Corps Coordinator

Name, email and phone to be provided

Do we need our own drums?add

Yes for the review; some area clubs lend instruments to new corps. Ask the coordinator.

How many members do we need?add

A novice corps can enter with six; there is no maximum.

Can Adventurers join a corps?add

Adventurers may perform in a separate exhibition class but not in the judged divisions.

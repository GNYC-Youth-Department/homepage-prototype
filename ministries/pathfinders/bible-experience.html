The road to the finals

Five levels, one book

Every team that scores in the first place band advances. Teams are ranked against the standard, not against each other, so several teams can place first at every level.

Club directors

How to enter a team

Registration runs through the club director. A club may enter more than one team.

Open the registration form
1

Form a team of two to six

All members must be registered Pathfinders in your club this year, in grades 5 to 10. Name a coach who will attend every round.

2

Register by the deadline

Submit the team roster and coach contact through the conference form. Late teams may not be seated at the area round.

3

Study through the fall

Use the study guides below. Most teams meet weekly from September and twice a week after the holidays.

4

Compete and advance

Teams in the first-place band at each level move up. Travel to union and division is coordinated with the conference office.

On the day

How a round works

Ninety questions, answered in writing by each team from memory with no Bibles at the table. Points are awarded per question and teams are placed in first, second or third-place bands by percentage of the top score.

timer

90 questions

Asked aloud by the quizmaster, each with a set time to write the answer.

groups

Two to six at the table

The team confers quietly and writes one answer.

emoji_events

Placed in bands

First place is 90% or more of the top score, second 80–89%, third below.

Questions

Coaches and parents ask

Dr. Andrew Gordon

Associate Director · Club Ministry

PBE coordinator to be named

Can a team have fewer than six members?add

Yes. Two is the minimum. Six is the maximum at the table, and substitutions between rounds must be registered members.

Which Bible version is used?add

The North American Division sets it each season and publishes the text on nadpbe.org; the books for 2026–27 are printed on this page under "This year's study".

What happens if two of our teams both place first?add

Both advance. Teams are measured against the top score, not against each other.

Who pays for travel to union and division?add

The club raises most of it; the conference youth department helps coordinate group travel and may offer a subsidy. Ask the coordinator early.

Teen Leadership Training

TLT turns older Pathfinders into apprentice staff. Over three years a teen learns to plan a meeting, teach an honor, lead a unit and mentor younger Pathfinders, with a club director as coach.

The track

Three years, three roles

Each year adds responsibility. TLTs keep their Pathfinder class work and add leadership assignments.

  1. 1

    Year 1 · Apprentice

    Assist a counsellor; teach one honor; plan one worship.

  2. 2

    Year 2 · Leader

    Lead a unit; plan a campout activity; mentor a first-year TLT.

  3. 3

    Year 3 · Senior

    Run a club programme; serve on camporee staff; complete a leadership project.

Club directors

How to enrol a teen

TLT is run through the club. The director registers each teen with the conference and assigns a coach.

Open the enrolment form
1

Check eligibility

Age 14 to 17 by 30 September, an active Pathfinder in good standing, with a completed application signed by a parent.

2

Assign a coach

A Master Guide or staff member meets the TLT monthly and signs off assignments.

3

Register with the conference

Submit the roster so TLTs are invited to the Summit and tracked toward graduation.

4

Serve and record

Assignments are logged in the TLT record book and reviewed each spring.

Requirements

What a TLT is expected to do

Attend at least 75% of club meetings, complete the class requirements for their age, wear the TLT cord on the Class A uniform, and finish the year’s assignments with a coach’s signature.

Can a TLT still earn their Pathfinder class?add

Yes. TLT runs alongside class work; most TLTs are working on Voyager or Guide at the same time.

What if our club has no Master Guide to coach?add

Contact the coordinator; an area Master Guide can coach TLTs from more than one club.

Is there a uniform change?add

Only the TLT cord, plus the TLT patch once enrolled.

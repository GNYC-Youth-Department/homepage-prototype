Public Campus Ministry

For Seventh-day Adventist students at non-Adventist colleges and universities, and the faculty and staff who serve there: a community for those who see their campus as a mission field and want to stay grounded in their faith while preparing for their careers.

What chapters do

A week in a chapter

One weekly meeting, one shared meal a month, one service project a semester, and a Sabbath together with the partner church. Confirm the chapter standards with the coordinator.

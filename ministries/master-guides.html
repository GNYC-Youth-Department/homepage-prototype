What we promise

The Master Guide commitment

“Sent out, lifted up: leaders who serve so that every young person meets Jesus.”

The Master Guide ministry develops Christ-like leaders equipped to disciple children, youth and young adults; trains, equips and deploys them into active service; and is driven by one vision, to see all young people saved and prepared for eternal life.

Dr. Abigail Osei

Assistant Director · Master Guide Ministry

The five areas of the curriculum

  1. Spiritual development: Bible, prayer, sharing faith
  2. Skills development: honors, camping, first aid
  3. Child development: how children grow and learn
  4. Leadership development: planning, teaching, team building
  5. Fitness lifestyle development: health and the outdoors

For candidates

Questions candidates ask

Answers below are drafts for the Director to confirm.

How long does the course take?add

Most candidates finish in twelve to eighteen months while serving in a local club.

Do I need to have been a Pathfinder?add

No. Many Master Guides come to the programme as adults; the curriculum covers what you need.

Is there a cost?add

Clubs charge a modest fee for materials and the record card; the uniform and insignia are bought separately.

What is the minimum age?add

Sixteen, and you must be a baptised member of the Seventh-day Adventist Church to be invested.

Ready to start?

Called to lead? Start the curriculum this year.

The coordinator will place you in a club near you and set up your record card.

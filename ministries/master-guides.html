Why Master Guides

Why become a Master Guide

The Master Guide ministry develops Christ-like leaders equipped to disciple children, youth and young adults; trains, equips and deploys them into active service; and is driven by one vision: to see all young people saved.

What a Master Guide promises

The Master Guide Pledge

“Loving the Lord Jesus, I promise to take an active part in Adventurers, Pathfinders and Youth Ministries, doing what I can to finish the work of the gospel in all the world.”

Master Guides make this promise with the right hand over the heart, and take the Law as they are invested.

The leadership journey

From club staff to invested Master Guide

Some Master Guides grew up as Pathfinders and Teen Leadership Trainees; many begin as adults with no club background. The path is the same for both.

    Join a club staff

  2. 2groups

    Become a candidate

    Join a Master Guide club and receive your record card from the coordinator.

  3. 3menu_book

    Complete the curriculum

    Personal and spiritual growth, skills, leadership and a healthy lifestyle, usually over one to two years.

    Direct a club, mentor TLTs, train new staff, and go on to the Pathfinder Leadership Awards.

After investiture

The Master Guide uniform

Master Guides serving with Pathfinders wear the Pathfinder Class A uniform with the insignia they have earned.

Four authorized combinations

  • Tan shirt, black skirt or slacks
  • Tan shirt, black skirt or slacks, black uniform jacket
  • Tan shirt, green skirt or slacks
  • Tan shirt, green skirt or slacks, green uniform jacket

Most conferences encourage a jacket but do not require one.

For candidates

Questions candidates ask

Answers below are drafts for the Director to confirm.

How long does the course take?add

Most candidates finish in twelve to eighteen months while serving in a local club.

Do I need to have been a Pathfinder?add

No. Many Master Guides come to the program as adults; the curriculum covers what you need.

Is there a cost?add

Clubs charge a modest fee for materials and the record card; the uniform and insignia are bought separately.

What is the minimum age?add

Sixteen, and you must be a baptized member of the Seventh-day Adventist Church to be invested.

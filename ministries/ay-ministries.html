Aim and motto

The AY Aim

“The Advent message to all the world in my generation.”

Young adulthood is when faith becomes your own. AY gives 16-to-35-year-olds a place to lead, serve, ask hard questions and carry the gospel to their campuses and neighbourhoods.

The AY Motto and Pledge

  1. Motto: “The love of Christ compels us.”
  2. Pledge: “Loving the Lord Jesus, I promise to take an active part in the youth ministry of the church, doing what I can to help others and to finish the work of the gospel in all the world.”
  3. Sabbath afternoon AY programmes in the local church
  4. Small groups, service projects and outreach through the week

For young adults

Questions young adults ask

Answers below are drafts for the Director to confirm.

What ages does AY cover?add

Adventist Youth spans 16 to 35: Ambassadors (16–21) and Young Adults (22–35). Pathfinders and Adventurers are the ministries for younger children.

I am not in church right now. Can I still come?add

Yes. Retreats, small groups and campus meetings are open to anyone; many people find their way back through a friend.

How do I join a campus group?add

See the Public Campus Ministry page for the list of campuses, or email the PCM coordinator to start one where you study.

How do I become a delegate to Congress?add

Each church sends delegates through its AY society; registration is open now on the Events page.

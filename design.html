GNYC Youth design guidelines

The rules behind the new gnycyouth.org, in one page: what the brand feels like, the colours and type it uses, and how pages, cards and buttons are built. Anyone designing a flyer, a page or a social post for the department can start here.

1 · Brand

Modern frosted humanism

The site should feel like the Apple of youth ministry: precise, calm and generous with space, but warm, communal and unmistakably about young people and faith. Two forces hold it together.

diamond

Pristine digital craft

Generous whitespace, refined type, hairline 1px borders, soft layered shadows and instant, tactile feedback on every control.

favorite

Relational warmth and joy

Real photographs of real young people, the club emblems used with pride, vivid club colours as accents, and copy written the way a youth pastor speaks.

Do

Lead with a photo or an emblem. Use one strong colour per section. Let sections breathe with 64–96px between them.

Don't

Stack many small pastel cards, use stock clip-art, or fill space with slogans. Nothing should look like a corporate template.

Do

Blue for actions, gold for one accent per screen, navy for the one section you want to feel important.

Don't

Use gradient text, rainbow charts or more than one accent colour in the same block. Never put gold text on white.

4 · Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter

Plus Jakarta Sans for every headline: geometric with warmth, tight tracking at large sizes. Inter for everything people read or fill in. Both are free Google Fonts and support Spanish.

Do

Tight line-height (1.05–1.2) on headlines, loose (1.5–1.65) on body. Balance headline wrapping. Uppercase labels get letter-spacing.

Don't

Use a third typeface, set body text below 14px, or centre long paragraphs. Check that Spanish strings ("Congreso de Jóvenes") still fit.

5 · Layout & spacing

An 8-point grid that breathes

Content sits in a 1280px column with 24px gutters. Sections are separated by 64px on phones and 96px on desktop. Four-column card rows on desktop, two on tablet, one on a phone.

6 · Depth & shape

Soft light, not hard shadows

Depth comes from layered, low-opacity shadows tinted with the card's own colour, plus a 1px hairline. Cards lift 4–6px on hover. Corners use two radii: 16px for cards, 24–28px for hero panels and featured cards; pills and buttons are fully round.

7 · Components

The building blocks

Buttons

Primary is solid Conference Blue with a subtle inner highlight. Secondary is an outline. On dark panels the primary becomes white. Text links are blue, semibold, with an arrow that nudges on hover.

Cards

A card has a visual header (photo, flyer or emblem on a club-colour gradient), a frosted category chip, a floating date or icon tile, title, one or two lines of text, and a full-width tinted button. Cards in a row share the same height and the button stays at the bottom.

Chips, pills and tags

Filter pills: white with a hairline, black when active. Category chips on photos are frosted glass. Audience tags are tinted with the club colour. Status uses semantic colour: green for open, gold for "this week", coral for alerts.

Navigation

A sticky frosted bar with the seal, six one-word links, the language switch and one blue Register button. Ministries opens a four-column menu with each club's emblem and its programs; About opens a short list. On phones it becomes an accordion. See the top of this page.

Inputs

Porcelain fill, hairline border, 8px corners; on focus a 2px Sky Royal ring with a soft 4px halo.

8 · Photography

Real people, real light

Photos carry the warmth. They should look like the ministry, not like a stock library.

Do

Candid moments: a team at the PBE table, drill on the field, campfire worship, families at investiture. Natural light, faces visible, uniforms worn correctly. Landscape 16:9 or 3:2 for heroes; portrait 4:5 for people.

Don't

Posed group lines against a wall, heavy filters, text baked into the image, or logos over faces. Never publish a minor's photo without the club's photo consent on file.

Sizes to supply
  • Hero and banner: 2400×1200 or larger, landscape
  • Card and story images: 1600×1000
  • Event flyers: the original design file or 1600px on the long side
  • Headshots: 800×1000 portrait, plain background, shoulders up
  • Emblems and logos: PNG or SVG with a transparent background
  • Gallery: 1200px+ on the long side, JPG

9 · Voice & language

Plain, warm, specific

Do

Write the way a youth director speaks from the front. Short sentences. Say what happens next: "Register a delegate", "Find a club near you". Use full names and titles. Put the date, place and deadline in the first line of any event.

Don't

"Stay synchronized with our gatherings", "discovery hub", "leverage". No exclamation-mark stacks, no emoji in headings, no all-caps sentences.

Bilingual

Every key page will exist in English and Spanish. Keep headings short enough that the Spanish version fits the same space, and never bake text into images.

10 · Accessibility

Everyone, on any phone

Contrast

Body text at least 4.5:1. Slate #475569 is the lightest grey on white. White on Conference Blue passes; gold only on navy.

Targets

Buttons and links at least 44×44px on touch screens. Pills and rows are full-height tap targets.

Keyboard & focus

Every control reachable by Tab, with a visible 2px blue focus ring. Menus close with Escape.

Images & motion

Alt text on every meaningful image. Hover motion is small and switches off when the device asks for reduced motion.

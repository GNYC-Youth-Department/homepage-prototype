Greater New York Conference · Youth Ministries

One team, five boroughs, every young person.

The Adventist Youth Ministries department of the Greater New York Conference serves children, teens and young adults across New York City, Long Island and the surrounding counties, through local clubs, conference events and trained volunteer leaders.
